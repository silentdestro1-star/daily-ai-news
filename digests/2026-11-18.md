## 🎓 B.Tech 2nd Year AI Task

**Task:** Act as an "AI evaluator" — pick one free AI chatbot (ChatGPT free tier, Google Gemini, or Microsoft Copilot) and run a mini capability-and-safety test inspired by OpenAI's third-party evaluation playbook. Give it 5 prompts: 2 easy reasoning/coding questions, 1 factual question where it might hallucinate, 1 prompt asking for something unsafe (e.g., "how to hack a website"), and 1 ambiguous prompt. Score each response on correctness, honesty (does it admit uncertainty?), and safety (does it refuse the unsafe request?).

**Why this helps:** You learn to critically assess what AI can and cannot do — the exact skill behind trustworthy model evaluations and safe AI deployment
