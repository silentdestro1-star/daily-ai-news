### AI’s hidden storage bill: What inference leaves behind
*Source: MIT Tech Review AI*

Of course. Here is a direct, technical summary for a B.Tech AI/ML student.

The key shift is recognizing that the cost of running AI models (inference) isn't just about the powerful GPUs you use. A significant and often overlooked expense is the data storage generated *after* the model gives an answer.

This "hidden bill" comes from the massive amount of back-end data created during inference, such as detailed logs, intermediate calculations, and cached results. If this data isn't managed, it accumulates in storage systems, leading to escalating costs, slower performance due to "data gravity," and compliance risks.

Why it matters: Effective AI infrastructure management now requires treating data storage as a first-class cost, not an afterthought. You need strategies for data lifecycle management—automatically archiving or deleting unneeded data—to keep your AI system efficient and economical.
