### Deep double descent
*Source: OpenAI Blog*

**Double descent** breaks the classical U-shaped bias-variance tradeoff: test error peaks at the **interpolation threshold** (where train error $\approx$ 0) then drops again as models grow larger, longer, or see more data.  
This happens because overparameterized models implicitly find smoother, lower-norm solutions that generalize better, rendering the "optimal capacity" heuristic obsolete.  
Practically, you can often skip heavy regularization—just scale the model or data past the peak instead of tuning constraints to avoid it.

[Read original](https://openai.com/index/deep-double-descent)
