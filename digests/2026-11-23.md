### Perplexity and Intelligence
*Source: OpenAI Blog*

Perplexity is just exponentiated cross-entropy loss — the geometric mean of the inverse probability your model assigns to the actual next token, so lower means less "surprise" on held-out text. The claim here is that it works as a shared yardstick for tracking model progress, which is partly true: it's cheap, tokenizer-independent *within* a fixed vocabulary, and correlates with downstream quality. The catch worth knowing is that perplexity is not comparable across different tokenizers or datasets, and it measures next-token prediction, not reasoning or task accuracy — so treating it as a capability benchmark conflates fluency with competence.
