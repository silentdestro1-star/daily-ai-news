### Estimating worst case frontier risks of open weight LLMs
*Source: OpenAI Blog*

This paper introduces "malicious fine-tuning" (MFT), a method to stress-test open-weight LLMs by fine-tuning them to maximize dangerous capabilities in dual-use domains like biology and cybersecurity. The key finding is that this process can significantly amplify the model's worst-case risks, demonstrating that open-weight models present a serious safety challenge. This matters because it shows that simply releasing a model with weights doesn't guarantee its safe use; malicious actors can weaponize it through targeted fine-tuning.
